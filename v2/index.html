How I work

Three positions

01

Framing before pixels

Most briefs I get are a symptom with a solution already attached. The first two weeks are usually spent proving the brief wrong, cheaply, before anyone has drawn a screen.

02

Design the policy, not the screen

On AI surfaces the interesting decisions aren't visual. How much can the system do unasked? What does it owe you when it's wrong? I write those as rules first, then the interface falls out of them.

03

Numbers with their caveats attached

Every metric on this site has a qualifier under it. Baselines move, launches confound each other, and a number without its limits is a liability in a design review.
